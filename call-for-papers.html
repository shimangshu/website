Call For Abstracts

Call for Contributions

Research, Innovation and Collaboration.

CIDSESRF-2027 invites researchers, academicians, scientists, engineers, students and professionals to submit abstracts related to the broad themes of the conference. The conference encourages interdisciplinary contributions connecting science, engineering, technology and society.

Abstract Submission

Participants are required to submit their abstract along with the registration form. The abstract should clearly present the objective, methodology, key findings and significance of the work. Each abstract will be reviewed by the conference committee for acceptance.

  • Abstract submission must be completed during registration.
  • The abstract should follow the prescribed format provided by CIDSESRF-2027.
  • Selected participants will need to submit full papers after acceptance of their abstracts.

Submission Process

01

Register

Complete registration and upload your abstract.

02

Abstract Review

Abstracts will be evaluated by the technical committee.

03

Full Paper Submission

Selected authors will receive full paper guidelines and submission instructions through registered email.

Important Dates

  • Abstract Submission: During the registration process
  • Abstract Acceptance: Will be communicated after review
  • Full Paper Submission Deadline: 26 January 2027

Publication and Proceedings

Accepted abstracts will be published in the official Conference Abstract Book of CIDSESRF-2027.

Accepted conference papers will be published in the Conference Proceedings with ISBN, subject to review and compliance with the prescribed format.

Further details regarding full paper submission and publication will be communicated to selected authors.
