CIDSESRF-2027 · 1927–2027

Call for Papers

Submit your research abstract and become a part of CIDSESRF-2027, the centenary conference of H.R.H. The P.O.W.I.E.T., Jorhat.

Call for Contributions

Research, Innovation and Collaboration.

CIDSESRF-2027 invites researchers, academicians, scientists, engineers, students and professionals to submit abstracts related to the broad themes of the conference. The conference encourages interdisciplinary contributions connecting science, engineering, technology and society.

Invitation for Contributions

Original research papers, review studies, applied research and innovative work relevant to the broad themes of CIDSESRF-2027 are invited.

Contributions should demonstrate scientific or technical merit and, wherever appropriate, highlight potential applications and implications for society, sustainability and resilient development.

Broad interdisciplinary participation

The conference encourages contributions that cross traditional disciplinary boundaries and connect science, engineering, technology and society.

Areas of Contribution

Submissions may address, but are not limited to, the following broad areas:

  • Science and emerging technologies
  • Materials science and nanotechnology
  • Mathematics and computational sciences
  • Sustainable development and resilience
  • Engineering and technology
  • Humanities, commerce and society

Abstract Submission

Participants are required to submit their abstract along with the registration form. The abstract will be reviewed by the conference committee for acceptance.

  • Abstract submission must be completed during registration.
  • The abstract should follow the prescribed format provided by CIDSESRF-2027.
  • Selected participants will be invited for full paper submission.
  • The full paper template and submission instructions will be shared with selected authors.
