About the Conference

Where disciplines meet, ideas converge.

The conference aims to provide an interdisciplinary platform for meaningful academic exchange, knowledge sharing and collaboration among researchers, educators, students and professionals.

The event brings together perspectives from science, engineering, technology and society, with a focus on emerging developments, innovation, sustainability and resilience.

Science · Engineering · Technology · Society · Sustainability

A platform for emerging ideas

The conference particularly encourages participation from young researchers and early-career academics, creating opportunities for presentation, interaction and networking.
