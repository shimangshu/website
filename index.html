About the Conference

Where disciplines meet, ideas converge.

CIDSESRF-2027 provides an interdisciplinary platform for researchers, faculty members, scientists, engineers, students and professionals to exchange knowledge and explore emerging developments across science, engineering and society.

Organised during the centenary year of H.R.H. The P.O.W.I.E.T., Jorhat, the conference celebrates a century of technical education while looking towards the challenges and opportunities of the future.
