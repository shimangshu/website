CIDSESRF-2027
A Centenary Conference · 1927–2027

Conference Themes

Areas of Interest

Six broad themes. One shared vision.

CIDSESRF-2027 welcomes contributions across disciplines, encouraging researchers and practitioners to connect emerging scientific knowledge with technological innovation and societal needs.

The themes listed below are broad areas of interest and are not exhaustive. Contributions addressing related interdisciplinary topics aligned with the scope of the conference are also welcome.

01

Frontiers in Fundamental and Applied Science

Advancing scientific understanding through fundamental discoveries and their applications towards solving contemporary challenges.

02

Science and Emerging Technologies for Sustainability and Resilient Future

Exploring scientific innovations and emerging technologies that contribute towards sustainable development, environmental security and societal resilience.

03

Emerging Technologies and Intelligent Engineering Systems

Addressing advancements in intelligent systems, automation, digital technologies and engineering solutions for future applications.

04

Sustainable Engineering, Innovation and Resilient Technologies

Highlighting engineering innovations and technological approaches for sustainable infrastructure, energy, industry and communities.

05

Society, Culture, Education and Human Development for a Resilient Future

Understanding the role of society, culture, education and human values in shaping an inclusive and resilient future.

06

Mathematical, Computational, Economic and Data-Driven Perspectives for an Inclusive Society

Integrating mathematics, computation, economics and data-driven approaches to address complex challenges and promote inclusive development.
